INTRODUCTION

Beyond the match result

OFF PITCH AFRICA is a dynamic Kenyan sports media and storytelling platform dedicated to amplifying the untold narratives of African athletes to the world. The platform goes beyond match results to explore the human stories, challenges, and triumphs that define life off the pitch.

Founded by Bonphace Odhiambo Otieno

Our Mission

To empower African athletes and sports stakeholders by providing a platform for authentic storytelling that inspires, informs, and drives the growth of sports across the continent.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading sports storytelling platform, recognized globally for shaping narratives that influence sports culture, policy, and development.

Our Community

Sports personalities, media gurus, fans of the game, data analysts, events organizers, strategic marketers and business personnel — connected by people like us.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Core Values

Authenticity

We are committed to sharing real experiences, unfiltered stories, told by us — in the Pitch and Off Pitch.

Excellence

Community

We are connected by people like us: sports personalities, media gurus, fans, data analysts, events organizers, strategic marketers and business personnel.

Innovation

From Off Pitch to across digital pathways, we thrive in innovating sustainable solutions for sports, events production, coverage and marketing.
